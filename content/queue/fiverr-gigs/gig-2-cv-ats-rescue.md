## FAQ (6)

1. **Do you use AI?**
   Yes, openly. AI-powered, human-reviewed: the rewrite is generated from YOUR CV and YOUR target job posts for this order only, then human-checked before delivery. Fiverr's rules (and mine) prohibit recycled bulk output — you will never receive a template that another buyer also got.

2. **Why don't you ask for my LinkedIn or job-board logins?**
   Because you should never give them to anyone. Password sharing breaches LinkedIn's and Indeed's terms and risks YOUR account. Everything here works from files and links you send — that's a feature, not a limitation.

3. **Will this make my resume "beat" the ATS?**
   I optimize what's controllable: parse-clean structure, standard headings, and honest keyword alignment with the specific posts you target. No one can promise interviews or shortlisting, and I won't. You'll receive a visibly stronger, correctly parsed, targeted document — that's the commitment.

4. **Will you add skills I don't have?**
   Never. Keyword targeting means expressing your real experience in the vocabulary of the job post. Fabricating experience would hurt you at the interview stage and is a hard no.

5. **What do you need from me?**
   Your current CV (any state, even a rough Word doc) and links or screenshots of 1–3 job posts you're targeting. That's it — the requirements form collects the rest in 2 minutes.

6. **What if I need full applications, not just the CV?**
   That's my main gig: tailored CV + custom cover letter for EVERY role you target, plus a tracker sheet — check "tailored job applications" on my profile. This gig is the right start if you want the foundation document fixed first.
