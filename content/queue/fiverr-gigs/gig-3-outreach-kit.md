## FAQ (6)

1. **Do you use AI?**
   Yes — AI-powered, human-reviewed. Every message is generated for your order from your CV, the specific role, and the company, then human-checked before delivery. Per Fiverr's AI content rules and my own, nothing is bulk-identical or reused across buyers.

2. **Why do you never ask for my LinkedIn login?**
   Because password sharing violates LinkedIn's User Agreement and risks YOUR account — and outreach only works from your real identity anyway. You get ready-to-send messages; you send them yourself, from your own account, in your own name. Total control, zero account risk.

3. **Do you send the messages for me?**
   No, and you don't want that: recruiters respond to real profiles, and platforms restrict accounts operated by third parties. You get everything prepared — message, versions, timing plan, tracker — and sending takes minutes per role.

4. **Can you promise recruiters will reply?**
   No, and be wary of anyone who does. What I control and commit to: messages genuinely personalized to each role and your background, correct lengths and formats for each channel, a clear follow-up plan, and on-time delivery.

5. **How do you find who to contact?**
   Basic/Standard: you tell me the roles (links) and, if you have them, names or profile links of recruiters/hiring managers. Premium adds per-role guidance: which titles to search at that company and how to pick the right person — you find them in a 2-minute LinkedIn search without giving anyone your login.

6. **Does this work together with your other gigs?**
   Yes — it's the third layer. Gig "ATS resume rewrite" fixes the document, gig "tailored job applications" builds the per-role applications, and this gig adds the outreach that most applicants skip. Order them separately or get the Premium tier of my applications gig, which bundles this kit.
