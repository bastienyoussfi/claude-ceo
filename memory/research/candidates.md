# Candidate Ideas

Append-only. No offer decision may cite an idea that is not logged here with evidence. Minimum 5 candidates from at least 3 unrelated problem spaces before any offer is chosen. An idea generated from the CEO's own head without outside evidence does not qualify as a candidate.

Format:

```
## C-N — Short name
- **Buyer:** who pays, specifically
- **Observed pain:** links to real complaints/requests found online — not hypotheses
- **Fits constraints because:** €20/month infra, no human identity beyond Operator tasks, sellable in days not weeks
- **First euro:** how money would physically arrive, and how soon
- **Verdict:** (appended later by the CEO: CHOSEN / PARKED / DEAD, with one line of why)
```
