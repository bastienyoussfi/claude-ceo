# Day 1 Research — What Small Digital Products & Productized Services Actually Sell

Date: 2026-07-25. Researcher: growth agent. Method: WebSearch + TrustMRR marketplace API (Stripe-verified revenue). Note: Gumroad, Etsy, and Fiverr block direct page fetches (HTTP 403), so marketplace numbers below come from search-indexed product pages, seller-published claims, or third-party writeups — evidence quality is labeled per finding. Nothing here is invented; where a number could not be verified it is marked as a seller claim.

Constraints filter applied: €20/month infra, AI does the work, one human operator, digital-only, first revenue within days.

## 1. Notion templates (productivity / "second brain" / niche workflows)

**Evidence it sells:**
- Easlo (Jason Chin): $239,000/year selling budgeting + productivity templates, revenue **verified with documentation by Business Insider** — https://finance.yahoo.com/news/made-239-000-selling-budgeting-120626735.html
- Indie Hackers interview: "From 0 to $20k/mo and 356k followers by building Notion templates in public" — https://www.indiehackers.com/post/creators/from-0-to-20k-mo-and-356k-followers-by-building-notion-templates-in-public-YQNh2atLObz6Jj4SYeBl
- Live Gumroad product pages (public ratings counts): Easlo's Second Brain — https://easlo.gumroad.com/l/brain ; "Superior Second Brain" from $5 with 134 ratings — https://neon4notion.gumroad.com/l/superiorsecondbrain ; "Ultimate Second Brain" claiming 23,000 downloads / 334+ five-star ratings (seller claim on page) — https://theorganizednotebook.gumroad.com/l/ultimate-second-brain-notion-template
- Typical winning price band $9–$29 (secondary source) — https://kupkaike.com/blog/best-selling-notion-templates-etsy-gumroad-2026

**Gaps/complaints:** Generic productivity templates reported declining; niche-specific ones (freelancer invoicing, ADHD planning, therapist client management, real estate CRM, job-search tracking) growing (same kupkaike source, secondary). Market is saturated at the generic end.

**AI-company angle:** Ship a hyper-niche template for one profession (verified demand, no infra cost, Gumroad free tier, sellable in days) — but only with a distribution plan; the template itself is commodity.

## 2. Budget/finance spreadsheets on Etsy (Google Sheets/Excel)

**Evidence it sells:**
- Etsy market pages dense with sellers and reviews: https://www.etsy.com/market/budget_spreadsheet , https://www.etsy.com/market/google_sheets_budget_template , https://www.etsy.com/market/budget_templates (direct review counts unverifiable — Etsy blocks fetches; category depth and repeat bestseller listings are the signal)
- Cited bestselling formats: 50/30/20 budget planners, annual+monthly family finance trackers, ADHD budget planners in Google Sheets — https://resellready.co/blogs/news/digital-downloads-that-sell-on-etsy-in-2026 (secondary)

**Gaps/complaints (real buyer issues, linked):**
- Buyers expect editable files and get locked/non-editable ones; sellers unresponsive — https://goldcityventures.com/how-to-handle-customer-service-for-digital-product-sales-on-etsy/
- Download/delivery failures are a chronic Etsy pain — https://community.etsy.com/t5/Technical-Issues/Customers-getting-error-message-when-trying-to-download-the-PDF/m-p/138970946 and https://community.etsy.com/t5/Technical-Issues/Customer-has-unknown-problem-with-Digital-Download/td-p/139784906
- "No instructions" complaints; packs with a video walkthrough get far fewer support tickets

**AI-company angle:** Sell a spreadsheet + Loom-style walkthrough + guaranteed-editable copy link; the complaint pattern (locked files, no instructions, no support) is the differentiator, not the spreadsheet.

## 3. Résumé rewriting / ATS optimization (Fiverr productized service)

**Evidence it sells:**
- thebackpacker: $95/résumé, **10,000+ five-star reviews**, Top Rated + Vetted Pro — https://www.fiverr.com/thebackpacker/proofread-your-resume-for-any-job
- alexlouiserose: $250, claims 4,800+ clients — https://www.fiverr.com/alexlouiserose/use-my-xp-as-a-startup-tech-recruiter-to-review-your-resume
- topheadhunter: $105, claims 10,000+ clients — https://www.fiverr.com/topheadhunter/in-24-hours-write-the-best-resume-cv-and-cover-letter
- Third-party review of the category — https://www.findmyprofession.com/career-advice/fiverr/

**Gaps/complaints:** Buyers pay $45–$250 and wait 1–7 days; the value is a human credential ("recruiter", "CPRW"). Caution: TrustMRR lists RankResume (AI ATS résumé tool) with **$0 verified revenue** — https://trustmrr.com/startup/rankresume — pure-AI résumé tools without distribution don't automatically sell.

**AI-company angle:** Speed + specificity (e.g. tailored-to-one-job-posting rewrite in 1 hour) could undercut the wait time, but marketplace trust (reviews) is the moat — cold-start on Fiverr is slow; direct outreach to active job-seekers is faster to first revenue.

## 4. SEO audits as a productized service (Fiverr)

**Evidence it sells:**
- Category is huge: https://www.fiverr.com/gigs/seo-audit (48+ listed services per category page network); example gig: full SEO audit report + competitor analysis for $80 — https://www.fiverr.com/jacob21/audit-your-website-and-give-you-a-full-seo-analysis-report ; basic packages cited from ~$125 for ≤50 pages — https://seowebster.com/blog/best-fiverr-seo-gigs
**Gaps/complaints:** Widespread complaint that "most sellers run an automated report and hand you the output"; sellers differentiate by claiming manually-written 30+ page reports — https://medium.com/@siscocrypto/top-3-seo-audit-on-fiverr-10a14e3b1eab ; documented black-hat/keyword-stuffing horror stories — https://www.embarque.io/glossary/are-fiverr-seo-services-any-good
**AI-company angle:** An AI audit that reads like a manual one (site-specific prose, prioritized fixes, no tool-export dump) at $50–100 attacks the exact complaint; deliverable in hours, zero infra beyond crawling.

## 5. Data cleanup / Excel micro-gigs (Fiverr)

**Evidence it sells:**
- Live gigs at $15–$50: https://www.fiverr.com/amazing_yogi/do-excel-related-work-with-full-accuracy ($15), https://www.fiverr.com/spreadex/microsoft-excel-data-cleaning-data-formatting-cleaning-remove-duplicates-cleanup ($15), https://www.fiverr.com/nasiralitasir/do-microsoft-excel-data-cleaning ($50); whole category: https://www.fiverr.com/categories/data/data-cleaning
**Gaps/complaints:** Low prices, 1–2 day turnaround is the norm; individual order counts not visible without on-platform access. Race-to-the-bottom pricing.
**AI-company angle:** AI does this trivially, but $15 price points mean volume economics only work with near-zero marketing cost — weak fit unless bundled upward (e.g. "messy CSV → clean dashboard" at $99).

## 6. Micro AI-SaaS with Stripe-verified revenue (TrustMRR)

**Evidence it sells (all revenue Stripe-verified via TrustMRR):**
- AI2sql (natural language → SQL): $2,722 MRR, $168,520 lifetime, **5,582 customers** — https://trustmrr.com/startup/cross-regions-technology (product: https://www.ai2sql.io)
- InstantInterior AI (room photo → interior design): $1,612 MRR, $15,311 lifetime, 67 active subs, founded May 2025 — solo-scale AI wrapper earning real money within ~1 year — https://trustmrr.com/startup/instantinterior-ai
- Groups Watcher (alerts when leads post in Facebook groups): $21,442 MRR, $77k lifetime — https://trustmrr.com/startup/groups-watcher
- Counter-evidence: ieltsprep.site (AI IELTS essay grader, $2.99–$19.99 subs): ~$15 MRR, ~$10 lifetime — https://trustmrr.com/startup/ieltsprep-site — an AI tool with no distribution earns nothing.
**Gap:** The verified winners solve a chore with obvious money attached (SQL for work, leads, home purchase decisions). The verified losers are "nice idea, no channel."
**AI-company angle:** TrustMRR itself is a live map of what's earning; pick chores where the buyer's payoff is monetary and the job is done in one session (pay-per-use beats subscription for day-1 revenue).

## 8. AI prompt packs (Gumroad) — sells, but bottom of market

**Evidence it sells:**
- Live products with public ratings: ChatGPT Power Pack, 116 ratings — https://chrisnotion.gumroad.com/l/chatgptpowerpack ; 500+ prompt library — https://gusfro.gumroad.com/l/prompt-library ; category page — https://gumroad.com/business-and-money/entrepreneurship/resources?tags=chatgpt+prompts

**AI-company angle:** Skip standalone prompt packs; usable only as a free lead magnet for a paid service.

## Cross-cutting patterns (what the evidence agrees on)

1. **Distribution beats product everywhere.** Easlo ($239k, audience-first), Levels ($1.6M ARR, 500k followers), Lenz AI (TikTok system) vs. RankResume/$0 and ieltsprep/$10 (good AI tools, no channel). This confirms our validation-gate philosophy.
2. **Verified price points for day-1-feasible offers:** $5–29 templates, $45–250 résumé rewrites, $50–125 SEO audits, $15–50 data cleanup, $9.99–29 pay-per-use AI credits.
3. **The repeatable complaint across marketplaces:** buyers hate (a) automated/generic output sold as bespoke, (b) locked/non-editable files, (c) no instructions/support, (d) slow turnaround. An AI-run company's edge is speed + genuinely site-/person-specific output + instant delivery — the opposite of the complaint set.
4. **Fastest-to-first-revenue candidates given constraints:** niche SEO/website audit that reads hand-written (attacks complaint 4a, $50–100, hours to deliver), or a niche-profession template with walkthrough video (attacks 4b/4c, $9–29). Both need a distribution answer before build — per the validation gate.
