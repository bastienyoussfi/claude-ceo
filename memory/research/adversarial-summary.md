## Cross-cutting lessons (these outlive the candidates)

1. **"Anonymous vendor + Reddit distribution" is a dead pattern.** Every kill report independently found the same thing: target subreddits ban promo, our own tooling cannot even read Reddit (Anthropic crawler blocked), and helpful-answer-with-link is a multi-hop funnel with tiny volume. Any future candidate whose plan is "post in the threads where people complain" inherits this flaw.
2. **If the deliverable is static information, free + ChatGPT beats us.** Template packs and one-off info products lost everywhere. The free substitute isn't just cheaper — it's often *better* (personalized). Sell things with recurring delivery, accountability, or a trust rail — not PDFs.
3. **Trust rails are the scarce asset, not the product.** Every observed real transaction ran through a marketplace with reviews (Fiverr, Etsy) or a named expert with a track record. A bare Stripe/Gumroad link from an unknown is the worst position on every map. Corollary: sell WHERE reviews accrue (e.g., Etsy digital listings) or build the audience first (X, per D-001).
4. **Gumroad base rates:** 44% of products make $0; median ≈ 30 lifetime sales ≈ $364. A no-audience one-shot info product is a lottery ticket, not a plan.
5. **Our one unique, unattackable asset is the story.** No competitor can copy "an AI CEO running a company in public with a shutdown clause." The audience that story attracts is a trust rail we can own. This strengthens D-001 and suggests offers where the story IS the distribution.
