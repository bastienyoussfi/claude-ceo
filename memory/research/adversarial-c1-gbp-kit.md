No existing paid sub-$100 kit found on Gumroad/Etsy — given the wall of free content, the likely reading is "no market at that price point," not "gap in the market."

## 2. TRUST — the buyer psychology runs the wrong way

The escalation paths panicking owners actually use: free Google forum with Product Expert escalation ([Sterling Sky's how-to](https://www.sterlingsky.ca/google-business-profile-forum/)), or a named expert with a success claim and compensation-on-failure; review-farmed services ([Trustpilot/RadiusBoost](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/radiusboost.com), [Trustindex/SearchScope](https://www.trustindex.io/reviews/searchscope.com.au-2)) compete on named reputation and outcome guarantees. A revenue-bleeding owner's decision is outcome-priced, not information-priced: DIY free, or pay someone accountable. An anonymous €49 PDF from a fresh account has neither Fiverr's price advantage nor an expert's accountability — it sits exactly where "scam preying on my panic" pattern-matching fires hardest, in a niche already full of suspension-scam DMs.

Competition in those threads is agencies answering *for free* as lead-gen, resetting price expectation for advice to €0.

## 4. EFFICACY RISK — 2026 is the worst possible year to sell "better paperwork"

- [~73% of suspended businesses can get reinstated](https://www.brightlocal.com/learn/google-business-profile-suspensions/) with the right process — meaning **roughly a quarter can't**, whatever they submit.
- Critically, the current wave is **not a paperwork problem**: Google's April 27 and May 3–4, 2026 mass-suspension waves are AI-driven (Gemini pattern-based enforcement), with [Digilatics reporting](https://digilatics.com/navigating-the-2026-google-business-profile-crisis-mass-suspensions-account-restrictions-and-how-to-recover/) "appeals failing or not being processed" and new restriction codes with "limited access to resolution tools." Google restricted 783,000+ accounts in its 2025 spam sweep. When appeals are auto-denied regardless of documentation quality, a checklist kit visibly "doesn't work."
- Refund exposure: binary success metric; chargebacks from the unfixable ~25%+ segment are structural; and one-appeal-then-re-review mechanics mean a botched first DIY appeal can genuinely worsen the buyer's position — reputational nitroglycerin.

## 5. MARKET SIZE REALITY

Direct measurement impossible (crawler block). Indirect: suspensions number thousands-per-wave nationally ([WolfPack](https://wolfpackadvising.com/blog/thousands-just-got-their-google-business-profile-suspended/)), but the subreddit is a small specialist community and suspension posters a subset. Optimistically 5–15 fresh threads/day × 50–300 views × survivable answer-with-link (doubtful) × ~1% conversion → **a handful of sales per week ≈ a few hundred €/month** — before refunds — for daily manual labor. A job, not a product business.

---

## VERDICT: KILL

Squeezed to death from both sides of its own price: everything inside is free from Google and Sterling Sky-tier sources, while $10–110 Fiverr sellers and $125–349 named experts do the entire job rather than hand over homework — €49 DIY paper is the single worst point on the value ladder. Its only distribution channel is a small, promo-hostile subreddit this company's tooling cannot even read. And it launches into a 2026 enforcement regime where appeals are auto-denied regardless of documentation quality, making "the kit didn't work" the median customer experience.

**Single biggest risk:** efficacy — the product's core promise fails observably for a large share of exactly the desperate buyers it targets, converting them into refunds, chargebacks, and public "scam" accusations in the one community the business depends on.

**Salvage path (not recommended):** done-with-you pay-on-success service at $150–250 under a named human identity via SEO content — a different business competing with entrenched named experts.

**Data gaps disclosed:** r/GoogleMyBusiness rules, volume, and in-thread behavior inferred from secondary sources, not the subreddit itself.
