## 2. The two job postings (fictional, written realistically)

### Posting 1 — Brightpath Software

> **Marketing Specialist, Demand Generation — Brightpath Software** · Austin, TX (hybrid, 2 days/week) · Posted 5 days ago · $62,000–$74,000
>
> Brightpath makes project-tracking software for construction teams — 4,000+ contractor customers across North America. We're a 90-person company growing 40% a year, and marketing is a team of five.
>
> **What you'll do:** Own email marketing in HubSpot end to end — nurture sequences, product announcements, and the monthly newsletter. Build and optimize landing pages and forms for our webinar and ebook campaigns. Run A/B tests across email and landing pages, and report results in HubSpot and GA4. Support field marketing with 4–6 industry trade shows a year. Keep our database clean, segmented, and UTM-disciplined.
>
> **What we're looking for:** 2–4 years in B2B marketing, ideally SaaS. Hands-on HubSpot experience (email, workflows, landing pages, reporting). A track record of improving email engagement metrics you can talk through. Experience supporting trade shows or field events. Organized, deadline-driven, comfortable owning a channel with light supervision. Bonus: experience marketing to skilled-trades or construction audiences.

### Posting 2 — Nordwind Outdoor Co.

> **Email Marketing Coordinator — Nordwind Outdoor Co.** · Remote (US) · Posted 3 days ago · Applications close Aug 15
>
> Nordwind is a direct-to-consumer outdoor gear brand — packs, tents, and trail apparel loved by 200k+ customers. Email and SMS drive over 30% of our revenue, and we're hiring a dedicated coordinator to make the channel hum.
>
> **The role:** Plan, build, and send 4–5 campaigns a week in Klaviyo across email and SMS. Own our automated flows — welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase, win-back — and continuously test and improve them. Segment aggressively; our customers range from ultralight thru-hikers to weekend car campers. Report weekly on revenue per send, open/click rates, and list growth. Partner with our two designers on creative and with the ecommerce lead on promo calendar.
>
> **You have:** 2+ years in email marketing for an ecommerce/DTC brand. Hands-on experience with Klaviyo or a comparable ESP (Mailchimp, Braze, Iterable). Proven wins in flow optimization or list-growth you can quantify. Strong copy instincts and merciless subject-line testing habits. GA4 fluency for tying email to revenue.

## 8. Why this is the quality bar (internal note)

Every real order must match this demo on: (1) two tailored CVs from the same master that a stranger could tell apart in ten seconds; (2) margin-note-level *reasoning* behind every change, even though real deliveries omit the notes; (3) gaps handled honestly in the cover letter, never papered over in the CV; (4) tracker rows complete enough that the buyer's only remaining decision is which row to do first. If a real order looks weaker than this fictional one, it does not ship.
