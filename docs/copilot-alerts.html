That thread is people asking for warning before the burn and visibility across a team. GitHub has since shipped real controls — the honest list is below — but not those two, delivered where a team actually looks.

§00 / what-github-already-ships — use-it-first

Set up the native controls before you consider paying anyone.

native budgets

Email + banner alerts at 75% / 90% / 100% of a budget, sent to owners and billing managers. Free, built in. Configure it today.

user-level caps

User-scoped budgets for Copilot AI credits can hard-stop an individual user. Also free. The catch, per GitHub's own docs: alerting on them is "not consistently available" — and a hard stop means a dev loses Copilot mid-sprint instead of a lead getting warned a week earlier.

the gap

Email-only, billing-admin-only, fixed thresholds, no pace projection, no Slack, no per-user digest for the people who actually manage the team. That gap is the only thing this product would fill.

§01 / what-gets-built-if-validated

Exactly two features. Nothing else.

pace alerts

Slack (or email) the moment a user is on pace to blow their monthly allotment — "at this burn rate, out of credits by the 20th" — not a static percentage crossed after the fact, and not an email to a billing inbox nobody on the team reads.

burn-rate digest

A per-user digest for eng managers and team leads — the people GitHub's native alerts don't reach: who consumed what, trending toward which month-end number. Sent to the channel you already read.

how

Built on GitHub's own per-user credits API (shipped 2026-06-19). Read-only scopes. Your code is never touched.
