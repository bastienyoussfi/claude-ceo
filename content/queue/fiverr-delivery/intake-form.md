# Intake Form — Fiverr Gig Requirements (F-1)

> **STATUS: DRAFT — pending Day 3 GO decision.**
> This is the buyer questionnaire, written to be pasted into Fiverr's gig "Requirements" section. Fiverr shows these after purchase; the delivery clock starts when the buyer answers. Questions are ordered so the mandatory ones come first, and every question explains *why* it's asked — buyers answer complete questions faster, and incomplete intake is the #1 schedule killer (SOP §2).
> Fiverr requirement types used: **File upload**, **Free text**, **Multiple choice**. Mark ✱ = set "answer required" in the gig editor.

---

## Q1 ✱ — Your current CV (File upload)

> Upload your most recent CV/resume (.docx, PDF, or a Google Docs link set to "anyone with the link can view"). It does NOT need to be polished or up to date — I work from the raw material. If it's missing recent work, add a note in Q7.

## Q2 ✱ — Your target roles (Free text)

> Paste your job links — one per line — OR describe what I should find for you.
>
> **Option A (recommended, fastest):** paste the links to the postings you want to apply to. Your package covers **3 (Basic) / 10 (Standard) / 30 (Premium)** roles. Links to the company's own careers page work best.
>
> **Option B:** if you'd rather I find the openings, write "FIND FOR ME" and give me: the job titles you're targeting (1–3), your industry preference if any, and your minimum salary if you have one. I only pick live postings, fresh within 14 days, matched to your location and work-authorization answers below.
>
> You can also mix: some links + "find the rest."

## Q3 ✱ — Seniority (Multiple choice, single answer)

> Which best describes your level?
> - Entry level / new graduate (0–2 years)
> - Mid-level (2–6 years)
> - Senior (6–12 years)
> - Lead / management
> - Director+ / executive

## Q4 ✱ — Location & work authorization (Free text)

> Three quick facts I never skip, because they decide which roles are worth your time:
> 1. City/region where you're based
> 2. Are you open to remote, hybrid, on-site, or relocation? (list all that apply)
> 3. In which country/countries can you work **without** visa sponsorship? If you need sponsorship, say so — I'll steer toward employers that state they sponsor.

## Q5 — Tone (Multiple choice, single answer)

> How should your cover letters and outreach messages sound?
> - Professional and warm (default — safe everywhere)
> - Formal and traditional (law, finance, government)
> - Direct and confident (startups, sales)
> - Creative with personality (marketing, media, design)
> - Match my CV's existing voice

## Q6 — Off-limits (Free text)

> Anything I should avoid? Common answers:
> - "Don't include my current employer's name in outreach messages"
> - Companies I must NOT be put forward to (e.g., my current employer, its subsidiaries)
> - Details to leave off the CV (photo, full address, graduation year, etc.)
> - Anything else you don't want mentioned anywhere
>
> Write "none" if nothing applies.

## Q7 — Extra ammunition (Free text)

> Optional but powerful: real wins that aren't (fully) on your CV — numbers, projects, awards, promotions, side work. "Grew the newsletter to 5k," "trained the new hires," "cut processing time in half." I can only use achievements you actually made — I never invent anything — so the more real material you give me, the sharper your documents get. Your LinkedIn URL is welcome here too (the public page only — see below).

## Fixed text under the questions (paste into the gig requirements intro)

> **What I will never ask you for:** passwords or logins to LinkedIn, Indeed, your email, or anywhere else. Everything I deliver works without me touching your accounts — you stay in control and press "apply" yourself (about 2 minutes per role with the kit I send). Anyone who asks for your job-board password is putting *your* accounts at risk of suspension.
>
> **Honesty policy, stated up front:** I tailor, reorder, sharpen, and quantify your real experience. I don't add degrees, jobs, dates, or skills you don't have — those requests will be politely declined (the rest of your order is unaffected). A CV that lies fails you at the first interview question.
>
> **How this order runs:** I confirm your materials are complete within hours (one consolidated message if anything's missing — never a drip of questions). Then you get: a tailored CV + cover letter for every role, an application tracker with every link and deadline, and recruiter/hiring-manager outreach drafts with a follow-up schedule. AI-powered, human-reviewed — and customized per role, every time.
