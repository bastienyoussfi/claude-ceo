# Replacement Scan — Day 2 (2026-07-26)

> Trigger: the Operator asked the CEO "is there not another idea that sells more, has more demand?" This file scans for offers the existing corpus (candidates C-1..C-13) MISSES, using a hard filter derived from every lesson so far. Research by a dedicated sub-agent, WebSearch evidence, 2026-07-26.

## The hard filter (all six required)

1. Buyer cannot self-build it (non-technical or time-starved — never developers; the C-10 lesson).
2. Recurring or accountable delivery — not static info (adversarial lesson 2).
3. A trust rail usable in week 1: marketplace with escrow/reviews, OR buyer pays before trust matters (emergency), OR the AI-CEO story credibly reaches this buyer.
4. Money attached — buyer loses real money without the delivered thing.
5. First euro physically possible ≤14 days from 2026-07-27 (€20/mo infra, Operator for accounts/payments, AI delivers, 0 followers).
6. Distribution does not depend on fresh-account Reddit comments (adversarial lesson 1).

17 shapes examined; 3 finalists below; 14 rejected with reasons at the bottom.

## F-1 — Done-for-you job-search service (reverse-recruiter-lite), sold on Fiverr

- **Buyer:** White-collar job seekers in the 2026 hiring crisis — time-starved, emotionally exhausted, non-technical. Every week unemployed is a week of burned salary and savings.
- **Observed pain (URLs):**
  - ~20% of professional job seekers have been searching **10–12 months**; some report **1,700+ applications with no result** ([Job Board Doctor, Apr 2026](https://www.jobboarddoctor.com/2026/04/10/white-collar-job-loss-gen-z/)).
  - LinkedIn now processes **~11,000 applications per minute, +45% YoY** ([Sunflower Talent Strategies](https://www.sunflowertalentstrategies.com/blog/the-uncomfortable-truth-about-hiring-in-2026)); the white-collar market is the one segment not healing ([Fortune, May 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/05/08/jobs-report-april-2026-ai-white-collar-layoffs-finance-wages/)).
  - People demonstrably PAY at every rung of a tall price ladder: Fiverr gigs at [$10](https://www.fiverr.com/muhammadzesh761/search-and-apply-1000-jobs-for-you), [$45/100 applications](https://www.fiverr.com/senboy96/apply-job-for-you), [$100](https://www.fiverr.com/jesse500/apply-to-jobs-on-your-behalf-to-companies) in a dedicated live category ([Fiverr "apply for jobs"](https://www.fiverr.com/gigs/apply-for-jobs), [career-counseling/search-apply](https://www.fiverr.com/categories/lifestyle/career-counseling/search-apply)); reverse recruiters charge **$150–$4,500+/mo**, e.g. $1,500/mo for 100 tailored applications/week ([Careerify pricing guide 2026](https://www.careerify.io/reverse-recruiter-cost), [Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/business-news/workers-are-paying-recruiters-thousands-to-find-jobs)).
- **Why the buyer can't self-build:** self-serve auto-apply tools exist, but they require operating them, produce untailored spam (the "spray and pray" the market now punishes), and the buyer is precisely the person with no energy left. The evidence that DFY beats DIY here: the Fiverr category and the $1,500/mo human services coexist with cheap tools.
- **Trust rail (week 1):** Fiverr escrow + an existing category buyers already search. We are not fighting a review moat the way C-4 died — gigs at $10–45 from low-review sellers populate page 1 of this category because demand surges faster than reviewed supply. Note the inversion: the corpus only ever treated Fiverr as the competitor's moat; it is also a rail WE can stand on.
- **Price:** intro €25–35 per weekly batch (50 tailored applications + live tracker sheet + weekly summary — the tracker/report is standard in the category); ladder to €99/mo recurring. Recurring is structural: the buyer re-orders every week until hired.
- **First-euro path:** Day 1–2 Operator opens seller account, gig live with intro pricing. First order: new-seller data says 3 days–4 weeks for a well-optimized gig, "some within days" ([Hustle Spire 2026](https://hustlespire.com/how-long-to-get-orders-on-fiverr/)). Realistic first euro **day 5–14** — inside the window, not guaranteed.
- **Biggest risk:** delivery mechanics — tailored applications may require access to the buyer's job-board accounts (credential handling; LinkedIn ToS-gray). Mitigation exists in-category (sellers deliver via email-apply + company career pages + tracker sheets), but this must be verified before choosing. Secondary: Fiverr cold-start variance (30–90 days is the bad tail).

## F-2 — French e-invoicing compliance setup for micro-entrepreneurs (Sept 1, 2026 wave)

- **Buyer:** French micro-entrepreneurs / TPE with no accountant. **10+ million economic actors** are covered by the reform ([economie.gouv.fr](https://www.economie.gouv.fr/tout-savoir-sur-la-facturation-electronique-pour-les-entreprises)).
- **Observed pain (URLs):**
  - From **1 Sept 2026** every French business — micro-entrepreneurs included, even those not charging VAT — must be able to RECEIVE e-invoices via a state-approved platform ([URSSAF official](https://www.urssaf.fr/accueil/actualites/facturation-electronique.html), [portail-autoentrepreneur](https://www.portail-autoentrepreneur.fr/academie/gestion-auto-entreprise/facturation/facture-electronique-obligatoire)). That is ~5 weeks after our window opens.
  - The free public portal (PPF) was **abandoned in Oct 2024** — everyone must now choose among **101+ commercial approved platforms** ([Konica Minolta on the PPF abandonment](https://digital-solutions.konicaminolta.fr/gestion-documentaire/article-portail-public-de-facturation/), [Qonto's list of approved platforms, June 2026](https://qonto.com/fr/blog/gestion-entreprise/facturation/liste-des-plateformes-agreees)). Choice paralysis is structural, not incidental.
  - Fear has a number: **€50 fine per non-compliant invoice, capped at €15,000/yr** under the 2026 finance law ([lamicrobyflo](https://lamicrobyflo.fr/facturation-electronique-micro-entreprise/)); entire guide/reassurance cottage industry already exists ([forum thread](https://www.forumfr.com/sujet1009848-la-facture-%C3%A9lectronique-imminente-pour-les-auto-entrepreneurs-ce-qu%E2%80%99il-faut-savoir-et-comment-s%E2%80%99y-pr%C3%A9parer.html)).
- **Why the buyer can't self-build:** it's not a build problem, it's a decision + configuration problem in bureaucratic French tax jargon: pick the right platform among 101 for their case, register, get into the annuaire, verify a test invoice arrives. Micro-entrepreneurs without accountants (the majority) have nobody doing this for them — experts-comptables serve their own clients first.
- **Trust rail (week 1):** deadline urgency (buy-before-trust dynamics strengthen every week toward Sept 1), Operator's real French identity and native French — this is a French-language arbitrage play where the anonymous-vendor penalty is lower because almost no polished DFY offer exists yet at the micro end (none found on ComeUp — checked, nothing surfaced).
- **Price:** €39–59 one-time "mise en conformité" (platform chosen for your case + registration + annuaire + verified test reception, documented) + optional €5–9/mo compliance watch through the 2027 emission deadline (recurring tail).
- **First-euro path:** Day 1–3: one-page FR offer + Stripe link + ComeUp listing (rail exists but ComeUp average first sale is **28 days** — too slow alone, [comparados test](https://www.comparados.eu/en/comeup-test/)); day 3–14: Operator's real-identity outreach in French freelance/AE communities and direct network (NOT fresh-account promo posting). Realistic first euro **day 7–14**, moderate confidence.
- **Biggest risk:** "I'll just take a free tier and wait" — some approved platforms offer free reception tiers ([treso2](https://www.treso2.com/actualites/abandon-de-la-plateforme-de-facturation-de-letat-focus-sur-les-plateformes-de-dematerialisation-partenaires-pdp/)), and the reception-only 2026 obligation is easy to procrastinate until the 2027 emission deadline; plus the reform has slipped before. The offer sells confusion-removal, and confusion-removal competes with doing nothing.

## F-3 — Chargeback response service for merchants OUTSIDE Chargeflow's coverage (PayPal/Square/regional PSPs) + VAMP-ratio watch

- **Buyer:** Small e-commerce merchants (Shopify/Woo/PayPal sellers — non-developers) hit by a dispute with a hard response deadline.
- **Observed pain (URLs):**
  - Merchants systematically lose disputes and money: fees $15–25 per chargeback plus product plus payment; documented $1,465 loss on one $800 order ([Shopify community thread, 43+ replies](https://community.shopify.com/t/why-are-we-losing-all-chargeback-disputes-even-with-good-customer-service/187858)).
  - The 2026 regulatory wave is real and unknown to its victims: Visa VAMP merchant threshold tightened **2.2% → 1.5% on April 1, 2026**, with **$8 per disputed transaction** and no warning tier; "most small business owners have never heard of it" ([Beancount.io, July 17 2026](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/07/17/visa-vamp-chargeback-threshold-drop-small-merchants-guide), [MRC](https://merchantriskcouncil.org/learning/resource-center/member-news/blog/2026/stricter-vamp-ratio-thresholds-are-now-in-effect-heres-how-to-stay-compliant)).
  - Emergency structure: PayPal gives sellers **10 days to respond or auto-lose** ([chargebacks911](https://chargebacks911.com/paypal-chargeback-time-limits/)) — the buyer pays before trust matters.
  - The gap is named by a third party: Chargeflow (25% success fee, free until win) **only works with Stripe and Shopify Payments — PayPal, Square, Adyen, Braintree, Worldpay merchants "cannot use Chargeflow at all"** ([RFP.wiki](https://www.rfp.wiki/payments-fraud/chargeback-management/chargeflow)).
- **Why the buyer can't self-build:** reason-code-specific evidence packs under a 10-day clock while running a store; the merchant doesn't know what a compelling response even contains (the Shopify thread is merchants doing it themselves and losing).
- **Trust rail:** emergency deadline (pay-before-trust) + a Fiverr gig as the storefront. Recurring: disputes recur, and a €9/mo VAMP-ratio watch (dispute-rate monitoring with the 1.5%/0.5% thresholds) is a true monitoring product with money attached — C-11's shape instantiated on a non-developer niche.
- **Price:** €29 per dispute response (48h turnaround) + €9/mo ratio watch.
- **First-euro path:** Fiverr gig + one-page offer; emergency buyers search at the moment of pain. First euro **day 7–14**, lower confidence than F-1 (search volume for the exact emergency term on Fiverr is unverified).
- **Biggest risk:** ChatGPT-substitute (adversarial lesson 2) — a merchant can paste the dispute into ChatGPT free; and Shopify shipped a native AI-powered dispute evidence form in Dec 2025 ([Shopify changelog](https://changelog.shopify.com/posts/uplift-disputes-evidence-form)) — the C-3 native-coverage kill pattern is already moving. The offer survives only for out-of-coverage PSPs and only as accountable done-for-you; this is the weakest finalist.

## Cross-cutting insights (new, not in the Day 1 corpus)

1. **Fiverr-as-OUR-rail inversion.** Day 1 research only ever encountered Fiverr as the review-moated competitor that kills us (C-1, C-4). But in categories where demand is surging faster than reviewed supply (job applications in the 2026 white-collar crisis), the marketplace is a rail a zero-reputation seller can stand on — escrow substitutes for reputation at €25–45 price points. New-seller cold start (3 days–4 weeks well-optimized) is the honest tax.
2. **French-language arbitrage.** The Operator's real French identity + native French is an unused asset. The e-invoicing wave (10M+ obligated actors, hard Sept 1 deadline, free public option killed) is the only platform wave found anywhere in this research whose victims are non-developers AND where being French-speaking is a moat. The Day 1 wave file (platform-waves-july-2026.md) only contains developer waves — this was its blind spot.
3. **Both top finalists monetize desperation with accountable delivery, not information.** The buyer in F-1 and F-3 pays under a clock (weekly burn / 10-day deadline); the deliverable is work performed + a report, not a PDF. This is the exact inverse of the five killed info products.

## Ranking

1. **F-1 Job-search DFY on Fiverr** — largest measurable demand of anything examined all week (11k applications/min, 20% searching ~a year), tallest price umbrella ($10 gig → $4,500/mo human), delivery is pure AI labor, and the trust rail already carries identical offers.
2. **F-2 French e-invoicing compliance setup** — 10M+ obligated buyers, a hard deadline 5 weeks out, near-zero DFY competition at the micro end, Operator-native language moat; weaker week-1 distribution rail is the honest gap.
3. **F-3 Chargeback response + VAMP watch (non-Chargeflow PSPs)** — real emergency rail and a genuinely new 2026 regulatory wave, but the ChatGPT-substitute and Shopify-native-AI risks mean it should only be attempted as an add-on experiment, not the primary bet.
