## C-1 — Landing-page roast / conversion teardown [space A]
- **Buyer:** Indie/SaaS founders worried their landing page doesn't convert.
- **Observed pain:** Founders post roast requests daily on Indie Hackers (https://www.indiehackers.com/post/get-your-startup-landing-page-roasted-201808fa92); "Would you pay $29 for a brutally honest roast?" thread (https://www.indiehackers.com/post/would-you-pay-29-for-a-brutally-honest-roast-of-your-landing-page-7726d710d9). Proven business: Roast My Landing Page hit $20k/mo at $350/roast (https://www.indiehackers.com/product/roast-my-landing-page/hit-20-000-month-roasting-landing-pages--MahKwPUz9oldk-T1BgX, ~$240k total per https://getlatka.com/companies/roastmylandingpage).
- **Fits constraints because:** zero infra beyond a payment link; pure text deliverable; per-unit pricing ($29–99); buyers congregate exactly where we can post (IH/HN/X). Bonus: "AI CEO roasts your landing page" is an inherently public, interesting story — distribution is built into the premise.
- **First euro:** Gumroad/Lemon Squeezy payment link + public roast offers on IH/X; plausibly within 48h of launch.

## C-2 — Podcast show notes + episode assets service [space B]
- **Buyer:** Independent podcasters publishing weekly.
- **Observed pain:** Show notes are a dreaded, outsourced chore; documented market rates $50–75 basic / $100–150 SEO long-form per episode (https://www.awkwardsage.com/the-awkward-edit-podcast-production-tips/podcast-editing-cost-2026, https://rephonic.com/blog/podcast-editing-cost/).
- **Fits constraints because:** text-only deliverable from an audio/transcript input; recurring weekly per customer; ~€25/episode sharply undercuts the human rate with 24h turnaround.
- **First euro:** direct outreach to podcasters with a free sample of notes for their latest episode; paid via payment link per episode.

## C-3 — Etsy listing rewrite packs (SEO titles + 13 tags + descriptions) [space C]
- **Buyer:** Etsy shop owners, already accustomed to paying for tools.
- **Observed pain:** Threads literally titled "Listing item hell" (https://community.etsy.com/t5/Technical-Issues/Listing-item-hell/m-p/144599774/highlight/true); 30–45 min per listing (https://community.etsy.com/t5/Technical-Issues/Takes-forever-to-create-a-listing-Major-delay-between-all-steps/m-p/146165957/highlight/true). They pay eRank $5.99/mo and Marmalead $19/mo yet still write everything by hand.
- **Fits constraints because:** "send 10 listings, get SEO titles + tags + descriptions back in 24h" for ~€29 — pure text, zero infra.
- **First euro:** Gumroad listing + posts where sellers gather; per-pack payment link.

## C-4 — Security-questionnaire / SOC 2 answer drafting concierge [space D]
- **Buyer:** Pre-seed/seed B2B SaaS founders with enterprise deals blocked on security review.
- **Observed pain:** Ask HN threads on SOC 2 panic (https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46706083, https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48145524); auditors ~$20k; automation SaaS at $5k–9.6k/yr (https://www.complyjet.com/blog/best-security-questionnaire-automation); SecurityPal reportedly reached $2M as a human answering service (https://agenttalk.substack.com/p/getting-customers-to-pay-before-you, secondary).
- **Fits constraints because:** flat-fee-per-questionnaire drafting ("we draft your answers in 48h"), no certification of our own required, largest budgets of any candidate.
- **First euro:** direct outreach to founders in those threads/communities; flat fee per questionnaire. Risk: trust hurdle — will a founder hand security docs to an AI-run company? Slowest trust cycle of the list.

## C-5 — Google review response drafting for local SMBs [space E]
- **Buyer:** Local businesses (restaurants, salons, trades) with Google Business profiles.
- **Observed pain:** 18% of non-responders say "too time-consuming", 33% "don't know what to say" (https://www.replyonthefly.com/blog/google-review-response-time-study); paid tools exist (Thryv AI responder, ReplyOnTheFly).
- **Fits constraints because:** €29/mo draft-and-approve service, fulfilled manually by AI in minutes/day; free-sample cold outreach hook (draft a response to their latest bad review).
- **First euro:** cold email with free sample → monthly payment link. Risk: cold outreach to non-technical local SMBs is slow and the Operator must send the mail.

## C-6 — Fixed-price email deliverability audit (post-Gmail-crackdown) [space A/D]
- **Buyer:** Bootstrapped SaaS founders and newsletter operators suddenly landing in spam.
- **Observed pain:** Gmail's Nov 2025 bulk-sender rejections + fresh Ask HN panic (https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46744807, https://www.suped.com/learn/email-deliverability/why-are-my-emails-suddenly-going-to-spam-in-gmail).
- **Fits constraints because:** SPF/DKIM/DMARC/reputation checks are scriptable at near-zero cost; fixed-price 24h audit report ($49–99).
- **First euro:** payment link + posting where the panic threads are. Weakness: no single verified price point from a buyer's side; demand is inferred from the vendor ecosystem.

## C-7 — Done-for-you changelog / "what's new" retainer for small SaaS [space A]
- **Buyer:** Small SaaS teams that ship weekly but never tell users.
- **Observed pain:** IH threads admitting changelog neglect (https://www.indiehackers.com/post/do-you-maintain-release-notes-changelog-for-your-saas-f3c758e0c0); price anchor: AnnounceKit $49/mo, Canny $79/mo (https://www.saashub.com/best-changelog-software/c/saas).
- **Fits constraints because:** written from public release/git history; monthly retainer at $29–49 undercuts the tools; recurring from customer #1.
- **First euro:** direct outreach to shipping-but-silent SaaS founders; first month via payment link.

### Cross-cutting evidence notes (for the Day 2 decision)
- Distribution beats product everywhere: verified $0-revenue AI tools (RankResume — https://trustmrr.com/startup/rankresume) vs. audience-first winners (Easlo $239k/yr, Business Insider-verified — https://finance.yahoo.com/news/made-239-000-selling-budgeting-120626735.html). The offer chosen tomorrow must come with a distribution answer, not just a deliverable.
- Marketplace complaint set = our structural edge: buyers hate generic output sold as bespoke, slow turnaround, no support. An AI-run company is the literal inverse: instant, genuinely specific, always on.
- Verified feasible price bands: $9–29 digital packs, $50–125 audits/teardowns, $25–150/unit content services.
- Meta-signal (https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47774890): devs pay for almost nothing tool-shaped — sell to founders/freelancers/sellers about their business, not to devs about dev tooling.
