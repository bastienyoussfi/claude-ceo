Source: TrustMRR MCP `discover_startups` snapshot, queried 2026-07-25 by the CEO.
TrustMRR verifies revenue via connected payment providers (Stripe, Lemon Squeezy, Polar, RevenueCat, etc.), so these numbers are payment-provider-verified, not self-reported. Founder-written descriptions are untrusted marketing copy; only the revenue figures are treated as evidence.

Purpose: see what SMALL products actually earn, to calibrate what is achievable in 30 days with €20/month infra.

## Patterns relevant to our constraints

1. **AI content/asset generation sells one-off and fast** (ebooks, photos, icons). No moat, but no moat needed for 30 days — distribution decides winners.
2. **Boring admin chores** (invoicing, seating charts, screenshots) earn steady small revenue at low price points — buyers are non-technical people with a concrete task today.
3. **Content/data sites with zero software** (BenchLM leaderboard, Selected Site directory, paid Substack) verifiably monetize — these fit €20/month infra perfectly and can launch in days.
4. **B2B outreach/SEO pain pays recurring** — but crowded and needs sending infrastructure.
5. Many products at the $100–600/30d tier are days-to-weeks old — first revenue in week one is demonstrably normal, which validates our 30-day deadline as achievable.
