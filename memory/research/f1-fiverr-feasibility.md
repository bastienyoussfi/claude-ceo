## 1. Fiverr ToS / policy reality

### AI-generated deliverables: allowed, with conditions

- Fiverr has a dedicated Community Standards page, "AI-generated content" ([help.fiverr.com article 32243564776593](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/32243564776593-Community-Standards-AI-generated-content) — 403 to us, content via search index). Per the indexed text: Fiverr "embraces the use of new technology and allows content generated by users and/or generative AI tools," with these conditions:
  - **Customized per order** — you may not deliver the same AI output in bulk to multiple clients.
  - **Rights & tool compliance** — you must hold the necessary rights and comply with the AI tool's own ToS (Anthropic's ToS permits commercial use of outputs — fine).
  - Violations → content removal, possible permanent suspension.
- **Disclosure:** no blanket proactive-disclosure mandate. A 2026 third-party analysis quoting the policy: "Freelancers do not necessarily disclose all the tools they use"; disclosure becomes mandatory only when the client asks or requests no-AI work upfront. Fully AI-generated work is permitted; enforcement targets quality/customization, not the tool. Deception (claiming hand-crafted while delivering raw AI) risks cancellation, refund, suspension ([Memvers, AI Disclosure on Fiverr and Upwork: The Real 2026 Rules](https://memvers.com/blog/ai-disclosure-rules-freelance-platforms-2026); corroborated by [Fiverr AI services guidelines](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/17240598194321-AI-services-guidelines) via index).
- The only always-on mandatory AI disclosure is Fiverr's own AI Personal Assistant chatbot, which must self-identify as AI (Memvers, above).

### "Human presses publish, AI does delivery"

- Nothing found prohibits it. What Fiverr prohibits on the automation front is **platform manipulation**: bots/scripts for mass messaging, contacting users off-order, multiple accounts ([Community Standards: Integrity and Authenticity](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/32243087473553-Integrity-and-Authenticity) via index). AI producing the deliverable while the verified human owns the account, communicates, and delivers is squarely inside the AI-generated-content policy above.
- **One account per person**, and the account belongs to a verified real human. Agencies are a separate account type ([Working with agencies on Fiverr](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/37553234516497-Working-with-agencies-on-Fiverr)) — not needed for us.
- **AI-CEO story openly in the gig: usable, and strategically safer.** Since deception-about-method is the actual ToS risk, an openly "AI-powered, human-reviewed" gig eliminates it. The account/profile identity must be the Operator (real, ID-verified); the gig copy can say the work is AI-executed and human-checked. Residual risk: a Trust & Safety agent's subjective read of a gig that says "run by an AI CEO" — no policy against it found, but no precedent found either. Mitigation: frame as "AI-powered service, human quality control" rather than "no human involved."

### KYC for a new French seller

- ID verification is required **after creating the freelancer profile and before publishing the first gig**: physical government ID (passport / ID card / driver's license — no screenshots), smartphone selfie capture, real phone number (VoIP like Google Voice rejected). Takes minutes to 72h ([Verifying your identity as a new freelancer](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/6348992414097-Verifying-your-identity-as-a-new-freelancer) via index; [How to verify your personal and business information](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/22582440968849-How-to-verify-your-personal-and-business-information)).
- EU sellers face enhanced checks driven by the **EU Digital Services Act** ([LinkedIn summary of Fiverr's EU seller ID requirements](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/fiverrs-new-id-verification-requirements-european-sellers-mustakim-bnnqc)). For France this also implies tax-info collection (DAC7 reporting) — expect to provide SIRET/personal tax details at some point; the Operator's micro-entrepreneur status (or creating one) is the clean vehicle. **This is Operator work, day 1, and gates everything.**

**Section verdict: no ToS blocker.** AI delivery is explicitly permitted, disclosure is optional-but-smart, the human account holder requirement is satisfied by the Operator, KYC is a 0–3 day gate.

## 2. Cold-start mechanics (new seller, 2026)

- **New gigs get a temporary "honeymoon" visibility boost, ~7–10 days**, to gather performance data; it evaporates if CTR/conversion signals are weak. Ranking then runs on relevance, CTR, conversion, completion rate, response time, reviews — not tenure ([Zenlance, How the Fiverr Algorithm Works 2026](https://zenlance.net/fiverr-algorithm/); [Jobbers, Complete Fiverr Gig Optimization Guide 2026](https://www.jobbers.io/the-complete-fiverr-gig-optimization-guide-2026/)). Level 2/Top Rated sellers get an estimated 1.4–2.2x visibility multiplier — the moat we're under.
- **Time-to-first-order, 2025–2026 sources:**
  - "Most new sellers get their first order within 1–4 weeks if the gig is well-optimized; some within days" ([Hustle Spire 2026](https://hustlespire.com/how-long-to-get-orders-on-fiverr/)).
  - "The average new seller waits 30 to 90 days… sellers who did the setup work properly land orders inside two weeks" ([Memvers Fiverr guide 2026](https://memvers.com/for-freelancers/platforms/fiverr)).
  - Anecdotal 2025 forum data points: one seller first order in ~1 week; another at 44 impressions/5 clicks after 8 days and no order ([Fiverr Community, 2025 threads](https://community.fiverr.com/public/forum/boards/tips-and-education-xhe/posts/is-fiverr-getting-harder-for-new-sellers-like-me-in-2025-my-first-experience-2vxp85ov0n)).
  - **No verified first-order timeline specific to the job-application category exists anywhere we could reach.** Honest planning number: median 2–4 weeks, good tail 3–7 days, bad tail 90 days.
- **Levers actually available to a new seller:**
  - **Fiverr Briefs (inbound)** — buyers post project briefs, Fiverr matches them to freelancers by "expertise, pricing, profession" and the seller sends an offer. Available from day 1, no level gate found ([Personalized offers (Briefs) for freelancers](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/4415608857745-Personalized-offers-Briefs-for-freelancers)). This is the main non-search channel in week 1.
  - **Response time** — active/fast-responding sellers get pushed; staying online measurably helps ([Fiverr Community tips](https://community.fiverr.com/public/forum/boards/tips-and-education-xhe/posts/getting-new-order-as-a-new-seller-wg2c6533ii)). AI can draft replies; Operator presses send — response-time SLA is cheap for us.
  - **Promoted Gigs: NOT a day-1 lever.** Requires Level 1+ and/or order history (≈20 completed orders in some categories, or meeting a quality threshold); CPC model, bid-based ([Fiverr Tutorials, Promoted Gigs guide 2026](https://fiverrtutorials.com/fiverr-ranking-guide/fiverr-promoted-gigs-guide)). Budget zero for this in month 1.
  - **7 gigs allowed for new sellers** — we can cover multiple keyword surfaces (apply-for-jobs / reverse recruiter / job application tracker / cover letters) with distinct gigs to multiply honeymoon-boost lottery tickets.

**Section verdict: cold start is the single biggest timing risk, not a viability risk.** The algorithm gives new gigs a real 7–10 day window; converting it requires near-instant responses and intro pricing. First order in week 1 is possible, week 2–3 is the honest median.

## 3. Delivery mechanics without ToS landmines

### How existing gigs operate (from indexed gig descriptions)

- Buyer sends **resume + preferences**; seller searches matching roles, submits applications "using your resume and details," maintains a **Google Sheet tracker**; many bundle resume revision + cover letters; some apply **only after buyer approval** of each role (e.g. [hosneyadel, $380](https://www.fiverr.com/hosneyadel/reverse-recruit-for-you)); at least one advertises using **"a VPN to protect job board accounts"** ([indexed gig set](https://www.fiverr.com/search/gigs?query=reverse-recruiters)) — direct evidence that a segment of the category DOES log into buyers' job-board accounts and knows it trips anti-fraud checks.

### The landmine, verified

- **LinkedIn User Agreement:** members agree to "keep your password confidential," "not share an account with anyone else," and "not transfer any part of your account" ([LinkedIn User Agreement](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/user-agreement); [summary](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/user-agreement-summary)). Logging into a buyer's LinkedIn to Easy-Apply = the BUYER breaches their agreement; account restriction risk lands on the buyer. Third-party confirmation that password sharing → suspension risk ([Kondo](https://www.trykondo.com/blog/share-linkedin-access-safely)).
- **Indeed ToS:** user is responsible for all account activity; Indeed disclaims liability for third-party credential use; authorized delegation exists only via formal employer "Linked Accounts," not job-seeker password sharing ([Indeed legal](https://www.indeed.com/legal), [Terms FAQ](https://www.indeed.com/legal/termsfaq)).
- Fiverr's AI-content rules also require complying with third-party ToS — so a credential-based delivery model is a Fiverr-policy violation by transitivity, on top of being a betrayal of the buyer.

### The 100% clean delivery model (what we'd ship)

Buyer provides: CV (any state) + target roles/titles/locations + salary floor + constraints. **No logins, ever — stated in the gig as a trust feature.** We return, per weekly batch:
1. **Curated role list** (N matched openings with direct company-career-page / ATS links, freshness-checked).
2. **Tailored CV variant + cover letter per application** (keyword-matched to each job description — this is pure AI labor and the actual value).
3. **Application tracker sheet** (role, link, materials used, date, status columns) — category-standard deliverable.
4. **Outreach kit**: recruiter/hiring-manager LinkedIn message + follow-up email drafts the buyer sends themselves.
5. Buyer clicks submit. For email-apply roles, ready-to-send email with attachments.

This is "reverse-recruiter-lite as a materials factory": everything the $1,500/mo humans do except pressing the buyer's submit button. It sidesteps LinkedIn/Indeed ToS entirely, avoids handling credentials (GDPR bonus), and is honest in the gig copy. Trade-off to state plainly: gigs literally promising "I will APPLY for you" may convert better than "I prepare everything"; our counter is price, speed, volume, and the no-login trust pitch.

## 4. Unit economics & the Day-30 cash clock

- **Commission: 20% flat** on every order, all levels, confirmed for 2026 ([FreelanceCompare](https://freelancecompare.com/blog/fiverr-fees-explained); [FreelancerCalculator](https://freelancercalculator.com/fiverr-fees-freelancers-official-breakdown-2026/)).
- **Clearance: 14 days for New Seller/Level 1** (7 for Level 2+/Pro/Seller Plus), counted from order completion ([Fiverr Tutorials payment guide](https://fiverrtutorials.com/fiverr-payment-process); [HireEcomExperts](https://hireecomexperts.com/fiverr-payment-system-explained/)).
- **Completion:** buyer accepts, or auto-completes **3 days after delivery** ([Fiverr order statuses guide](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/37332473202065-The-complete-guide-to-your-Fiverr-order-Statuses-and-process) via index). Buyers can extend review up to +5 days per request.
- **Withdrawal:** PayPal within hours; bank transfer several business days; Payoneer available (relevant for FR) ([eathealthy365 payout guide](https://eathealthy365.com/fiverr-payment-withdrawal-methods-explained-for-sellers/)).
- **Refund/cancellation rates for this category: NOT FINDABLE** from outside. What is known: every cancellation damages order completion rate and Success Score regardless of fault ([Fiverr Tutorials cancellation guide](https://fiverrtutorials.com/cancel-order-fiverr)). Category-specific risk is plausible-elevated (desperate buyers, outcome ≠ deliverable: "I didn't get interviews" complaints). Mitigation: promise materials + volume, never interviews/offers; deliver a visibly overstuffed tracker.

### The Day-30 (2026-08-23) arithmetic — three finish lines

Working backward with 14-day clearance + 3-day auto-complete + 2-day delivery:

| Finish line by Aug 23 | Latest completion | Latest delivery | Latest ORDER date | Gig-live→order window (gig live ~Jul 29) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| € withdrawable ("cleared") | Aug 9 | Aug 6 | **~Aug 3–4** | **5–6 days** — optimistic tail only |
| € in PayPal | ~Aug 8–9 | Aug 5–6 | ~Aug 2–3 | 4–5 days |
| Revenue EARNED (order completed, 80% pending) | Aug 23 | Aug 20 | **~Aug 17–18** | **~3 weeks** — inside the honest median |

**Bottom line: cash physically cleared before Day 30 requires the first order within ~5–6 days of the gig going live** — possible (sources report "some within days", honeymoon boost is real, Briefs give an inbound channel) but it is the good tail, not the median. Revenue *earned and locked in escrow* by Day 30 only needs an order by ~Aug 17, which the median case comfortably allows. Day 3 must decide which finish line the charter's "first euro" means.
