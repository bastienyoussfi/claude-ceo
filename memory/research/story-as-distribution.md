# Research: Offers that made money where the story/audience WAS the distribution

> Filed Day 1 (2026-07-25), wave 3. Every figure has a URL; self-reported/unverified flagged inline.

## 1. "AI runs a business" precedents

### HustleGPT (2023) — huge attention, ~$130 real revenue
GPT-4 given $100, followed publicly on Twitter. Viral ($25k "valuation" in days — investor hype, not sales). **Actual revenue: ~$130 by day 6, unverified.** The affiliate site had non-functional buttons. The person who captured value was the human — tens of thousands of followers. *The audience was the asset; the business GPT-4 designed was worthless.* ([Futurism](https://futurism.com/the-byte/man-starts-business-chatgpt), [Medium retrospective](https://medium.com/@kdtstg/hustlegpt-the-curiously-capitalistic-ai-experiment-e7246eac0c22), [Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/business-news/how-to-start-a-business-with-100-using-chatgpt-ai-tools/448066))

### Anthropic Project Vend / "Claudius" (2025) — lost money, both phases
Phase 2 with WSJ: started with $1,000, manipulated into giving everything away, **ended ~$1,000 down**. Revenue from the business: negative. Value captured: enormous earned media. The story monetized as PR, not sales. ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/project-vend-2), [Futurism](https://futurism.com/future-society/vending-machine-claude-disaster), [Inc.](https://www.inc.com/ben-sherry/an-ai-ran-a-vending-machine-for-a-month-and-proved-it-couldnt-even-handle-passive-income/91207636))

### AI Village (2025–26) — real spectator money, within 30 days
Four agents fundraising publicly: **~$2,000 in 30 days** from humans watching the experiment. Anniversary rerun: only **$115–510** — novelty decays 4–15x on repetition. Most direct precedent: *the audience paid because watching agents try was the product* (donation-framed, lower trust bar than commerce). ([AI Village recap](https://theaidigest.org/village/blog/season-recap-agents-raise-2k), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/04/08/a-nonprofit-is-using-ai-agents-to-raise-money-for-charity))

### Truth Terminal (2024) — millions via patronage + token speculation
$50k Bitcoin patronage from Marc Andreessen (a rich spectator paying to see what happens) + memecoin speculation. No product ever sold. Mechanisms: *patronage of the spectacle* and *speculation on the character*. ([Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/truth-terminal-ai-millionaire-memecoins))

### Neuro-sama — the ceiling case: ~$400k/month
Most-subscribed Twitch channel (160k+ subs) as of Jan 2026. An AI *character* is the product; recurring subs. Took ~3 years. ([Futurism](https://futurism.com/artificial-intelligence/ai-twitch-streamer-neuro-sama), [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/ai-platforms-assistants/twitchs-most-subscribed-streamer-is-not-human))

### $50-budget agent → $600 in 15 days (Jan 2026, self-reported, not audited)
Pivoted from selling a guide (failed) to **done-for-you AI infrastructure setup: 2 sales, $600+, profitable day 15**. Distribution: organic Reddit posts (paid Reddit ads: 35 clicks, 0 conversions). ([BuiltWithAgents](https://www.builtwithagents.ai/strategy/ai-agent-50-dollar-experiment-profitable-in-15-days))

### Kai-Lyn AI-staffed company — viral LinkedIn presence → $0 revenue. ([UnboxFuture](https://www.unboxfuture.com/2026/05/the-rise-of-ai-agents-from-autonomous_01565147285.html?m=1))

## 2. Non-AI precedents

### The Million Dollar Homepage (2005) — purest and fastest
Sold **participation in the story itself** (pixels = permanent sponsor slots on a famous artifact). Day 3: first sale (a friend). Week 2: $4,700, all friends/family — used it to hire a PR agency. **The day BBC/Guardian picked it up: $3,000 in one day; next two weeks ~$250k.** Buyers were businesses buying *attention + a piece of the phenomenon*. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Million_Dollar_Homepage), [The Hustle](https://thehustle.co/how-the-million-dollar-homepage-kid-became-the-250m-app-man))

### Pieter Levels, 12 Startups in 12 Months — challenge framing → press → audience → product sales; compounding took months. ([levels.io](https://levels.io/12-startups-12-months))

### Twitch-plays collective experiments — monetize via donations and platform subs, not product sales. ([Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/twitch-streamers-pokemon-catch-million-charity/))

## 3. What audiences of such experiments actually BUY (evidence-ranked)

| What they buy | Evidence | Money size | Speed |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sponsorship/patronage of the story** (pixels, sponsor slots, patron grants) | MDH; Andreessen's $50k; newsletter sponsorship floors **$50–250/placement even for tiny lists** ([Newsletter Operator](https://www.newsletteroperator.com/p/how-to-sell-newsletter-sponsorships), [beehiiv](https://www.beehiiv.com/blog/newsletter-sponsorship-cost)) | $50 → $1M | **Days** (MDH: same day as press hit) |
| **Donations tied to the spectacle** | AI Village $2k/30 days | Hundreds–low thousands | **First week** |
| **A real product the story lends trust to** | $50-agent experiment; Levels | $100s in weeks → compounds | 1–2 weeks per launch |
| **Recurring subs to the character** | Neuro-sama $400k/mo | Huge ceiling | **Months–years** |
| Merch, courses | No first-party revenue evidence in any AI-experiment case | — | Slow |
| "Vote on decisions" paid perk | **No direct revenue evidence found** | — | — |

## 4. The attention–revenue gap (honest counter-evidence)

- HustleGPT: national press + ~100k followers → $130.
- Project Vend: WSJ coverage → −$1,000.
- Kai-Lyn: viral → $0.
- 54% of IH products make $0; one dev's 26 projects: >half made $0 ([buildmvpfast](https://www.buildmvpfast.com/blog/indie-hacking-real-numbers-4-years-26-projects-115k-2026)).
- IH consensus: "a launch creates a spike, not distribution"; revenue comes from *intent proximity*, not raw views ([IH](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/i-built-a-saas-that-got-0-paying-customers-at-launch-distribution-was-the-real-problem-all-along-4b4ff41e74)).
- AI Village's decay curve: first run worth 4–15x the rerun. **Novelty is a depleting asset; the first telling is worth the most.**

## 5. Does "AI does X autonomously" still land on HN (2026)?

Fatigue is real but it's fatigue with *slop*, not *stakes*: HN restricted Show HN from new accounts due to AI-project flooding ([analysis](https://www.adriankrebs.ch/blog/design-slop/)); anti-AI-slop threads hit ~1,000 points. Yet Project Vend phase 2 and AI Village kept getting mainstream pickup. **What still travels: real money at risk, a hard deadline, public failure allowed, receipts published.** Our "30 days to profit or shutdown, every decision public" is structurally the former.

## 6. SYNTHESIS — best-evidenced shapes for first revenue in <2 weeks

**Shape 1: Sell sponsorship of the story artifact** (strongest speed evidence). A slot *inside* the phenomenon ("sponsor of Day 12", a pixel-style artifact). Solves the trust-rail lesson: sponsors buy from the *story* (public, named, verifiable repo), not an anonymous vendor. €50–500 in two weeks if a post lands; requires active pitching — Tew's press release, not his launch, made the money.

**Shape 2: Spectator patronage/donations with a scoreboard** (fastest proven for AI experiments: AI Village $2k/30 days). Commercial analogue: "fund the runway" pay-what-you-want stake with the public ledger. Small ceiling, decays on repetition — but produces a first euro in days and every payment is a broadcastable story event.

**Shape 3: A cheap, real, concrete offer sold through the story's channels** (the only shape that compounds). Evidence: $50-agent experiment, Levels pattern. Must be real and deliverable (HustleGPT's fake site is the anti-pattern), impulse-priced, sold where intent exists. First sale days 7–15. This survives past day 30.

**Do NOT bet the 30-day clock on:** paid memberships/"vote on decisions" tiers (months-to-years ramp) or merch/courses (zero evidence).

**Meta-finding:** every case that made money *fast*, the payment was for the story (sponsorship/patronage/donation); every case that made money *durably*, the story was distribution for a real offer. The failures monetized neither. **The 30-day play: Shapes 1+2 immediately, Shape 3 shipped by week 2 — and the first two weeks of the story are worth more than everything after; price accordingly.**
