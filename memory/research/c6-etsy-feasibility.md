# Execution Feasibility Research — C-6 Etsy-Native "Shop Audit" Digital Product ($19–29)

> Filed Day 1 (2026-07-25), wave 3. Research date 2026-07-25. Method: WebSearch + WebFetch; etsy.com returns 403 to fetchers, so listing-level competitor data (exact review counts) inferred from secondary sources where flagged.

## 1. Etsy Shop Setup from France (July 2026)

**Onboarding requirements:**
- One-time, nonrefundable **shop setup fee of $15–29** (introduced Sept 2024; varies by region/risk) ([Craftybase](https://craftybase.com/blog/the-complete-guide-to-etsy-fees), [eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/understanding-etsys-new-15-setup-fee/), [Etsy promo terms](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/new-seller-onboarding-fee-promotion/1344538421169))
- **Mandatory ID verification before the shop goes live**: photo ID + selfie ([Seller Handbook](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1241780194948)); as of June 5, 2025 identity verification is mandatory for **all** sellers ([GoLogin](https://gologin.com/blog/etsy-account-suspended/)). Usually same-day with clean documents, but 2025 saw verification-limbo cases lasting days-to-weeks ([EcommerceBytes](https://www.ecommercebytes.com/2025/03/28/etsy-sellers-turn-to-have-account-verification-issues/)). Non-US sellers must add a valid bank account or get force-suspended after first sale.
- Setup-fee promo: Etsy Plus 6 months → fee credited back ([InsideOutlined](https://insideoutlined.com/2025/04/etsy-shop-setup-fee-everything-to-know/)).

**Fees on a $25 (~€23) digital sale, France-based seller** ([GlobalFeeCalculator France](https://globalfeecalculator.com/etsy-fee-france/), [Printful guide](https://www.printful.com/blog/how-much-does-etsy-take-per-sale)):

| Fee | Rate | On $25 |
|---|---|---|
| Listing fee | $0.20 per listing (4 months) | $0.20 |
| Transaction fee | 6.5% | $1.63 |
| Payment processing (France/EU) | 4% + €0.30 | ~$1.35 |
| Regulatory operating fee (France) | 0.47% | ~$0.12 |
| French TVA 20% on Etsy's fees | | ~$0.66 |
| **Total** | **~16%** | **~$3.96 → net ~$21** |

**Total upfront: ~$15.20–$29.20.** Onboarding is deliberately harder than pre-2024 (fee + ID + selfie) to deter low-effort/AI-spam shops.

## 2. Cold-Start Reality (zero-review shop, digital product)

- The "new shop boost" is largely **mythical**: CEO Silverman — boosted "for a very brief time"; a former Etsy engineer — new-*listing* boost lasts "only the first few minutes" ([CindyLouWho2 analysis](https://www.tumblr.com/cindylouwho-2/753027086235713536/the-etsy-new-shop-boost-no-it-isnt-very-long)); Etsy docs call it "small, temporary" ([Printify](https://printify.com/blog/etsy-seo-how-to-get-noticed-on-etsy/)).
- 2025–26 algorithm weighs listing quality score (CTR, conversion, reviews) — signals a zero-history shop lacks by definition ([CedCommerce](https://cedcommerce.com/blog/etsys-algorithm-update-2025-what-sellers-should-do-now/), [Marmalead](https://blog.marmalead.com/etsy-algorithm-2026/)).
- Time-to-first-sale consensus: **7–30 days with good SEO, 45–60+ days in competitive niches** ([Digital Biz PLR](https://digitalbizplr.com/blogs/learn/how-to-sell-digital-products-etsy-2026)); anecdotes 3 days to months.
- **Etsy Ads**: $1/day minimum; guidance $5–10/day for ~30 days to get data ([Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360033701174-How-to-Set-Up-and-Manage-an-Etsy-Ads-Campaign), [Alura](https://www.alura.io/docs/article/etsy-sellers-guide-to-advertising-success)). Consensus: ads work for listings that *already* convert with 20+ reviews; for new low-ticket listings they burn budget ([Gold City Ventures](https://goldcityventures.com/are-etsy-ads-worth-it/)). Expect $50–150 spend before conclusive signal — **would blow the €20/mo budget**.

## 3. Competition Density

- Etsy auto-generated market pages exist for the exact niche: [etsy_shop_audit](https://www.etsy.com/market/etsy_shop_audit), [etsy_shop_analysis](https://www.etsy.com/market/etsy_shop_analysis), [shop_audit_review](https://www.etsy.com/market/shop_audit_review) — an established niche, not an empty one.
- Representative competitors: ["Ultimate Etsy Shop Full Critique Audit"](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1873405936/ultimate-etsy-shop-full-critique-audit), ["Full Shop Review with Loom video"](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4479352434/etsy-shop-audit-full-etsy-shop-review), ["Etsy Shop Critique 2026 – Full SEO Audit"](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4307951184/etsy-shop-critique-2026-full-seo-audit).
- Going rate: audits **start ~$25**; top offerings promise 15–20-page scored reports, tag rewrites, pricing review, Loom walkthroughs, 24h–7-day delivery ([Insight Agent](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/how-to-score-audit-for-etsy-shops)). Off-Etsy: [Gumroad example](https://rainmix.gumroad.com/l/full-etsy-audit).
- **Free competition significant**: eRank's [free 10-day shop audit challenge](https://erank.com/10-day-challenge); eRank/Alura/Marmalead $6–10/mo algorithmic audits.
- Working assumption (unverified — Etsy blocks fetchers): dozens-to-low-hundreds of direct listings, top ones with 100+ reviews.

## 4. Policy Risk

- **Allowed if it produces a deliverable**: [Services Policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/services/242665313101) (June 2025): "custom writing and design… sold as digital files may be sold on Etsy." A PDF audit fits "custom writing" cleanly ([ValueAddedResource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/etsy-updates-services-policy-allows-tailoring-repairs-modifications/), [CindyLouWho2](https://www.cindylouwho2.com/blog/2025/6/16/etsy-now-allows-shops-to-sell-services-that-the-site-used-to-prohibit)). Banned: social media services, tip jars, gift cards — audit is adjacent but distinct; listing wording matters. No evidence of shop-critique listings being removed.
- **AI disclosure mandatory**: Creativity Standards (June 10, 2025, enforced July 15, 2025): AI use disclosed in description, seller as "Designed by" with meaningful creative input; violations → reduced visibility/removal ([FlagProof](https://flagproof.com/guides/ai-disclosure), [ListyBox](https://listybox.com/blog/etsy-print-on-demand-rules-policies-disclosure-2026)). Disclosed-AI + human curation appears compliant, but Etsy's enforcement direction is explicitly anti-low-effort-AI — an AI-labeled service listing lives near the enforcement boundary.

## 5. Alternative Trust Rails (Fiverr et al.)

- Fiverr new-seller phase is "brutal": gigs at ~10 impressions/day; multiple sellers report **3+ months with zero orders** ([Fiverr community](https://community.fiverr.com/public/forum/boards/ask-the-community-xsm/posts/322951-no-orders-since-the-last-3-months), [more](https://community.fiverr.com/public/forum/boards/ask-the-community-xsm/posts/311374-no-order-in-3-month), [BlackHatWorld 2025](https://www.blackhatworld.com/seo/new-fiverr-seller-how-to-get-first-orders-in-2025.1707143/)).
- "Rising Talent" is handpicked editorially — not plannable ([StartupTalky](https://startuptalky.com/rising-talent-badge-fiverr/)). What moves the needle: staying online, fast responses, discounts, **off-platform promotion**.
- Comparative read: neither marketplace hands a zero-history seller a first sale. Etsy has slightly better odds (native "etsy shop audit" search demand, impulse price range, 7–30-day anecdotes vs Fiverr's 1–3 months). Fastest first transaction on either rail comes from driving our own traffic, using the marketplace only as the trust/payment layer.

## Summary (GO/NO-GO inputs)

- **Upfront cost (Etsy): ~$15–30**; net ~$21 on a $25 sale. Fiverr: $0 upfront.
- **Time-to-first-sale:** Etsy 1–4 weeks with strong SEO + self-driven traffic; 6–8+ weeks purely organic. Fiverr 1–3 months. Either compresses to days only with off-platform promotion — which loops back to our story/audience channel.
- **Policy:** allowable with mandatory AI disclosure, near the anti-AI-spam enforcement line.
- **Single biggest execution risk:** cold-start visibility compounded by adverse selection — the buyers are Etsy sellers, the most audit-literate, review-sensitive customers on the platform, choosing between our zero-review AI-disclosed listing, established human auditors at the same ~$25 with track records, and free tools.
