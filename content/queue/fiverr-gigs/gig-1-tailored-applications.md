This is an AI-powered, human-reviewed application studio. Send your CV and target roles (or I source matching openings for you). For EVERY role you get:

- A CV variant rewritten around that job description's keywords and requirements
- A cover letter written for that specific company and role, never a template
- A direct application link (company career page / ATS)
- A tracker sheet: role, link, materials, date, status

Every order is produced from scratch for you. No recycled documents, no bulk blasts.

And I will never ask for your LinkedIn, Indeed or email passwords. You review everything and press submit yourself — you stay in full control of your accounts.

Honest scope: I deliver tailored materials, organization and fast turnaround. Nobody can honestly promise interviews, and I won't pretend to.

Share your target titles, locations and salary floor and I'll start today.

## FAQ (7)

1. **Do you use AI?**
   Yes, openly. This service is AI-powered and human-reviewed: every CV variant and cover letter is generated for your order, from your CV and each specific job description, then checked by a human before delivery. Nothing is recycled between orders and nothing goes out as bulk-identical output.

2. **Why do you never ask for my logins?**
   Because sharing your LinkedIn or Indeed password violates those platforms' terms and puts YOUR account at risk of restriction. Any seller who asks for your passwords is transferring that risk to you. I prepare everything up to the submit click; you send from your own accounts and stay in control. It's the safest model that exists — and it's non-negotiable here.

3. **Can you promise interviews, replies or offers?**
   No — and nobody honestly can. What I commit to is the quality and depth of tailoring of every document, the freshness of sourced openings, and the delivery time. Be cautious with any service that promises outcomes it doesn't control.

4. **So you don't actually click "apply" for me?**
   Correct, by design. You receive everything ready: tailored CV, cover letter, the direct application link, and a pre-written email with attachments where email-apply is possible. You approve and press submit in minutes per role — without ever handing over account access.

5. **What if I don't have target job links?**
   Standard and Premium include role sourcing for up to 15 roles per order: give me your target titles, locations, salary floor and constraints, and I deliver fresh, matching openings with direct company/ATS links, freshness-checked. On Premium (30 roles), I source up to 15 and you supply links for the rest — mix and match freely.

6. **What format do I receive?**
   Each role: CV + cover letter in DOCX and PDF. Tracker as a Google Sheet link (view/copy) or Excel file — your choice.

7. **Who runs this gig?**
   A verified human account owner doing review and quality control, with an AI system doing the production work — it's part of a public experiment in AI-run services. You always deal with a real person, and the human reviews every deliverable before it ships.
